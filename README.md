1. Project Overview

This project analyzes an Orders dataset using Structured Query Language (SQL). The purpose is to examine sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, subcategories and regional performance.

The project involves retrieving, filtering, grouping and analyzing data to identify meaningful patterns and trends that help businesses understand their performance and make informed decisions.

2. Business Objectives
Understand the structure and contents of the Orders dataset.
Examine sales and profit generated by different product categories.
Identify the regions with the highest and lowest sales.
Determine the average sales per order for each customer segment.
Identify high-value orders with sales greater than $1,000.
Identify the five most profitable product subcategories.
Determine product categories that generated more than $10,000 in total profit.
Generate business insights and recommendations from the findings.
3. Dataset Description

The Orders dataset contains business transaction records relating to customer orders, product sales and profitability.

Field	Description
Row ID	Unique row identifier
Order ID	Unique identification number assigned to each order
Order Date	Date the order was placed
Customer Name	Name of the customer
Ship Date	Date the order was shipped
Ship Mode	Shipping method used
Customer Segment	Classification of customers into different segments
Region	Geographical area where the order was made
Product Category	General classification of products
Product Subcategory	More specific classification of products
Sales	Revenue generated from each order
Profit	Profit earned from each transaction
Quantity	Number of products ordered

6. SQL Analysis and Key Findings
Business Question 1: Data Overview

Objective: Retrieve the first 10 records from the Orders table to understand the dataset structure.

sql
SELECT TOP 10 *
FROM Orders;

Observation: The query returns the first 10 records, giving an overview of the dataset's columns, structure and the type of information available for analysis.

Business Question 2: Overall Business Performance

Objective: Calculate total sales and total profit.

sql
SELECT
    SUM(Sales)  AS TOTAL_SALES,
    SUM(Profit) AS TOTAL_PROFIT
FROM Orders;
TOTAL_SALES	TOTAL_PROFIT
2,297,201.07	286,397.79

Observation: The business is profitable, generating about 2.3 million in sales and 286,397.79 in profit.

Business Question 3: Regional Performance

Objective: Determine which region generates the highest total sales.

sql
SELECT
    Region,
    SUM(Sales) AS TOTAL_SALES
FROM Orders
GROUP BY Region
ORDER BY TOTAL_SALES DESC;
Region	Total Sales
West	$725,458
East	$678,781
Central	$501,240
South	$391,722

Observation: West recorded the highest sales, followed by East. South recorded the lowest.

Business Question 4: Product Category Performance

Objective: Calculate total sales and total profit for each product category.

sql
SELECT
    Category,
    SUM(Sales)  AS TOTAL_SALES,
    SUM(Profit) AS TOTAL_PROFIT
FROM Orders
GROUP BY Category;
Category	Total Sales	Total Profit
Office Supplies	719,046.99	122,490.88
Furniture	741,999.98	18,451.25
Technology	836,154.10	145,455.66

Observation: Technology is the best-performing category in both sales and profit. Furniture generates the lowest profit despite having higher sales than Office Supplies.

Business Question 5: Customer Segment Analysis

Objective: Determine the average sales per order for each customer segment.

sql
SELECT
    Segment,
    AVG(Sales) AS AverageSales
FROM Orders
GROUP BY Segment
ORDER BY AverageSales DESC;
Customer Segment	Average Sales
Home Office	$240.97
Corporate	$233.82
Consumer	$223.73

Observation: Home Office has the highest average sales per order, followed by Corporate. Consumer is lowest.

Business Question 6: High-Value Orders

Objective: Identify all orders with sales greater than $1,000.

sql
SELECT *
FROM Orders
WHERE Sales > 1000;

Observation: The query filters the Orders table to show only transactions above $1,000, helping identify high-value transactions.

Business Question 7: Product Profitability

Objective: Identify the five product subcategories generating the highest total profit.

sql
SELECT TOP 5
    ProductSubCategory,
    SUM(Profit) AS TotalProfit
FROM Orders
GROUP BY ProductSubCategory
ORDER BY TotalProfit DESC;
Product Subcategory	Total Profit
Copiers	$55,617.90
Phones	$44,516.25
Accessories	$41,936.78
Paper	$34,053.34
Binders	$30,221.64

Observation: Copiers generated the highest profit, followed by Phones and Accessories. Binders was lowest among the top five.

Business Question 8: Profitability Threshold

Objective: Identify product categories that generated more than $10,000 in total profit.

sql
SELECT
    ProductCategory,
    SUM(Profit) AS TotalProfit
FROM Orders
GROUP BY ProductCategory
HAVING SUM(Profit) > 10000;
Product Category	Total Profit
Technology	$145,455.66
Office Supplies	$122,490.88
Furniture	$18,451.25

Observation: All three categories exceeded $10,000 in profit. Technology was highest and Furniture lowest.

7. Business Insights
Technology is the strongest-performing category. It generated the highest sales and profit, making a significant contribution to financial performance.
The West region has the strongest sales performance. It recorded the highest revenue of all regions.
Home Office customers have the highest average order value. This segment may present an opportunity for targeted marketing.
High sales do not always mean high profitability. Furniture generated substantial sales but far lower profit than Technology and Office Supplies, which may indicate higher costs, discounts or lower margins.
Copiers is the most profitable subcategory. It is an important contributor to overall profitability.
All product categories exceed the $10,000 profit threshold. Their contributions differ, but all met the target.
8. Recommendations
Strengthen Technology sales through effective marketing, product availability and customer engagement.
Investigate the South region's low sales and consider targeted promotions and improved marketing.
Develop strategies for the Home Office segment, such as targeted offers and customer retention programs.
Review Furniture profit margins by examining costs, pricing strategies and discounts.
Prioritize high-profit subcategories such as Copiers, Phones and Accessories by maintaining stock and growing sales.
Monitor sales and profit regularly to spot changes in performance and make timely decisions.
